# Spaced repetition for DSA

Revisit concepts after increasing intervals rather than rereading them repeatedly in one sitting.

## Starting schedule

Try 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after the initial learning session.

| Result at revision | Suggested action |
|---|---|
| Independent derivation and correct implementation | Extend the interval |
| Correct idea, minor implementation issue | Keep a moderate interval |
| Needed a hint | Shorten the interval |
| Could not explain the approach | Relearn the prerequisite and revisit soon |
| Recognised but could not reproduce | Treat as not yet mastered |

## Do not turn the schedule into a burden

If you have many overdue problems, prioritise:
1. Foundational concepts used by many other topics.
2. Problems where you previously needed substantial help.
3. Recently learned concepts at risk of being forgotten.
4. Representative problems rather than every near-duplicate.

Use the tracker to make the next action visible.
