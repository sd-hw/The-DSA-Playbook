# Active recall for DSA

Active recall means trying to retrieve an idea from memory before looking at notes or a solution.

## What to recall

For a problem:
- What is the key observation?
- Which data structure or pattern applies, and why?
- What is the invariant?
- What is the brute-force approach?
- Why is the optimised approach correct?
- What are the time and space costs?
- Which edge cases matter?

For a concept:
- Define it in your own words.
- Explain when it is useful.
- State its limitations.
- Give a small example.
- Compare it with a related technique.

## A simple test

Close your notes and explain the approach on a blank page. If you cannot explain the idea without looking, you have identified what to revise.
