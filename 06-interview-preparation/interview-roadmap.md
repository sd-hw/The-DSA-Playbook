# Interview preparation roadmap

This is a flexible framework, not a fixed-duration guarantee.

## Stage 1 — Foundations

Review complexity, arrays, strings, hashing, linked lists, stacks, queues, recursion, trees and graphs.

**Exit check:** you can explain basic operations, trade-offs and common use cases without relying on memorised code.

## Stage 2 — Pattern practice

Work through a structured roadmap such as NeetCode 150. Track independent attempts, hints and revision outcomes.

**Exit check:** you can recognise relevant properties in unfamiliar questions and justify your chosen approach.

## Stage 3 — Deeper problem solving

Practise harder variations, dynamic programming, graph algorithms, heaps, greedy reasoning and less familiar patterns.

**Exit check:** you can reason about constraints, correctness and trade-offs under time pressure.

## Stage 4 — Interview simulation

Use the mock interviewer prompt. Practise clarifying questions, communicating while solving, testing and responding to hints.

**Exit check:** you can solve and explain problems in a realistic interview setting.

## Stage 5 — Broader engineering skills

DSA is only one part of software engineering. Depending on the role, prepare computer science fundamentals, databases, operating systems, networking, design, debugging, projects and behavioural interviews.

Adapt the depth to the role and verified interview expectations.
