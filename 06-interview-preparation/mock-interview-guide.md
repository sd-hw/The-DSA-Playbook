# Mock interview guide

## Simulate realistic conditions

- Choose a quiet 35–50 minute block.
- Use a blank editor.
- Avoid autocomplete or AI assistance if the goal is a no-AI simulation.
- Read the problem once, then ask clarifying questions.
- Communicate your reasoning.
- Test your implementation manually.

## Two useful modes

### No-AI mode
Use this to measure independent problem-solving, communication and implementation.

### AI-assisted mode
Use AI only as an interviewer or hint provider. Do not let it write the solution. This tests whether you can use assistance without surrendering ownership of the reasoning.

## Debrief

Write down:
- What I understood quickly.
- Where I got stuck.
- What hint helped.
- Whether the final code was correct.
- What I would do differently next time.
- Which topic should be revised.
