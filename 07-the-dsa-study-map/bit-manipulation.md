# Bit manipulation

## Learn
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 5, *Bit Manipulation*, begins p. 112. Its contents list bit facts and tricks, two's complement, right shifts, and common get/set tasks.
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** no dedicated bit-manipulation chapter appears in the contents.

## Focus on
Binary representation, bitwise AND/OR/XOR/NOT, shifts, masks, setting/clearing/toggling bits and signed integer representation.

## Practise
Use the Bit Manipulation section of a current practice roadmap.

## Check your understanding
- What does XOR return for equal and different bits?
- How can a mask test whether a bit is set?
- What is the difference between logical and arithmetic right shift?
- Which assumptions about signed integers or shift widths are language-dependent?
