# Heaps and priority queues

## Learn
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** Chapter 12, *Heaps*, printed pp. 361–382. The contents identify binary, binomial and Fibonacci heaps.
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 4 includes binary heaps (min-heaps and max-heaps), around printed p. 103.

## Focus on
Heap-order property, min-heap vs max-heap, insert, peek, extract, heap construction and using a priority queue to repeatedly retrieve the highest- or lowest-priority item.

## Practise
Use NeetCode Heap / Priority Queue problems, especially Top-K, merging sorted sequences and scheduling-style questions.

## Check your understanding
- Why is a heap not the same as a fully sorted array?
- What is the complexity of peek, insertion and extraction for a binary heap?
- When is a priority queue the right abstraction?
- Why might a theoretically faster specialised heap not be the practical choice?
