# Arrays and strings

## Learn
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** Chapter 3, *Arrays*, printed pp. 66–114; Chapter 4, *Strings*, pp. 115–137.
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 1, *Arrays and Strings*, begins p. 88. Its listed subtopics include hash tables, ArrayList/resizable arrays and StringBuilder.

## Focus on
Array indexing and traversal, insertion/deletion trade-offs, multidimensional arrays, string representation, character operations, frequency counting and the relationship between arrays and hash-based lookup.

## Practise
Use the official NeetCode roadmap's Arrays & Hashing, Two Pointers, Sliding Window and Prefix Sum areas where relevant. The exact roadmap can evolve; use its current topic list.

## Check your understanding
- When is an array better than a linked list?
- What does a hash map improve, and what are its costs?
- Why can a sliding window be valid for one range problem but invalid for another?
- Can you explain the invariant maintained by your two pointers?

## Revision
Re-implement a frequency-map problem, a two-pointer problem and a window problem without looking at old code.
