# Graphs

## Learn
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** Chapter 13, *Graphs*, printed pp. 383–423.
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 4, *Trees and Graphs*, begins p. 100; its contents list graphs and graph search. CTCI's Advanced Topics section also lists topological sort and Dijkstra's algorithm.

## Focus on
Vertices and edges, directed vs undirected graphs, weighted vs unweighted graphs, adjacency lists/matrices, visited-state management, BFS, DFS, connected components and dependency ordering.

## Practise
Use the official NeetCode Graphs section. Add problems involving traversal, cycle detection, connected components, topological ordering and shortest paths.

## Check your understanding
- When would you choose an adjacency list over a matrix?
- Why must traversal track visited vertices?
- When does BFS give a shortest path?
- What property is required for topological ordering?
