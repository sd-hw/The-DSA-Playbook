# Hashing and hash tables

## Learn
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** Chapter 15, *Hashing and Collision*, printed pp. 464–488.
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 1, *Arrays and Strings*, lists hash tables as a subtopic (p. 88).

## Focus on
Hash functions, buckets, collisions, chaining, open addressing, linear/quadratic probing, double hashing and rehashing.

## Practise
Use NeetCode Arrays & Hashing. Practise frequency maps, membership tests, grouping by keys and detecting duplicates.

## Check your understanding
- Why can two keys collide?
- What is the difference between chaining and open addressing?
- Why is hash-table O(1) usually an average-case statement?
- What happens as the load factor increases?
