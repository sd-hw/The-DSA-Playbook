# Complexity and algorithm analysis

## Learn
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** Chapter 2, *Introduction to Data Structures and Algorithms*, pp. 43–65; section 2.8 covers time and space complexity, including worst/average/best/amortised cases and time-space trade-offs. Sections 2.9–2.12 cover asymptotic notation.
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Introduction Chapter VI, *Big O*, printed pp. 38–59. Contents include time and space complexity, dropping constants/non-dominant terms, amortised time, logarithmic runtimes and recursive runtimes.

## Focus on
Input-size modelling, dominant terms, nested and sequential work, logarithmic loops, recursion trees, auxiliary space and amortised analysis.

## Practise
Analyse every problem you solve. State assumptions and distinguish worst-case, average-case and amortised complexity.

## Check your understanding
- Why is O(3n + n log n) simplified to O(n log n)?
- What does a loop that halves its search space contribute?
- How do recursive calls affect time and stack space?
- What is the difference between worst-case and amortised complexity?
