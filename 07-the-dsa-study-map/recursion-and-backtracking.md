# Recursion and backtracking

## Learn
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** Chapter 7, section 7.7.4, *Recursion*, printed p. 243; Appendix C, *Backtracking*, p. 514.
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 8, *Recursion and Dynamic Programming*, begins p. 130. Its contents list how to approach, recursive vs iterative solutions, and dynamic programming/memoization.

## Focus on
Base cases, recursive state, progress toward termination, call-stack usage, choosing among alternatives, undoing choices and pruning.

## Practise
Use NeetCode Backtracking. Practise subsets, permutations, combination search and grid/path exploration.

## Check your understanding
- What is the base case?
- What does each recursive call represent?
- Does every call make progress toward termination?
- Which state must be undone when backtracking?
- What pruning condition is safe?
