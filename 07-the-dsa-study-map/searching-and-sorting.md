# Searching and sorting

## Learn
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** Chapter 14, *Searching and Sorting*, printed pp. 424–463.
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 10, *Sorting and Searching*, begins p. 146.

## Focus on
Linear and binary search, sorting properties, comparison-based sorting, stability, in-place behaviour and how sorted input enables efficient algorithms.

## Practise
Use NeetCode Binary Search and relevant sorting-based problems. Also practise sorting as a preprocessing step for two pointers and interval problems.

## Check your understanding
- What condition makes binary search valid?
- What is the loop invariant in your binary-search implementation?
- Compare merge sort and quicksort at a high level.
- When does sorting first make a problem easier, and what is the added cost?
