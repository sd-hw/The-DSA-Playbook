# Dynamic programming

## Learn
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 8, *Recursion and Dynamic Programming*, begins p. 130; the contents explicitly list Dynamic Programming & Memoization on p. 131.
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** the contents do not identify a dedicated dynamic-programming chapter. Use its recursion section (Chapter 7, §7.7.4, p. 243) as a limited prerequisite, not as a complete DP resource.

## Focus on
State definition, recurrence/transition, base cases, evaluation order, memoization, tabulation and space optimisation after correctness is established.

## Practise
Use NeetCode 1-D DP and 2-D DP. Extend into subsequence, grid, knapsack-style and other patterns as your level grows.

## Check your understanding
- What does dp[i] or dp[i][j] mean in plain language?
- What choices lead to the current state?
- Why are the base cases sufficient?
- Is the dependency order valid?
- Can you derive the recurrence before coding?

## Coverage warning
The two uploaded books do not provide a complete modern DP curriculum. CTCI gives an introductory treatment; supplement it with a dedicated current resource and substantial practice.
