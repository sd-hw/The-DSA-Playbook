# The DSA Study Map

> **Want to learn a topic? Here's where to study it, what to practise and how to check that you understand it.**

The Study Map connects the uploaded book editions to interview-oriented practice. It is designed to reduce resource-hopping and make the next learning step obvious.

## How to use a topic page

For each topic:

1. **Learn:** read the relevant Reema Thareja section for structured explanations and representations.
2. **Apply:** read the related CTCI chapter and attempt selected questions.
3. **Practise:** solve relevant problems in the official NeetCode roadmap or LeetCode.
4. **Explain:** use the Socratic tutor to explain the approach, invariants and complexity.
5. **Retain:** revisit the problem without looking at your old solution.

You do not need to finish one book before starting the next. Follow a topic across the resources.

## What the books do not fully cover

The two uploaded books are not a complete modern interview curriculum. You will need additional practice or references for topics such as advanced dynamic programming, greedy proofs, interval patterns, union-find, advanced graph algorithms and modern language/library usage. The map marks these gaps instead of pretending the books cover everything.
