# Stacks and queues

## Learn
- **Reema Thareja, 2nd ed.:** Chapter 7, *Stacks*, pp. 219–252; Chapter 8, *Queues*, pp. 253–278.
- **CTCI, 6th ed.:** Chapter 3, *Stacks and Queues*, begins p. 96.

## Focus on
LIFO and FIFO behaviour, array and linked representations, push/pop/peek, enqueue/dequeue, queue variants, expression processing and using one structure to implement another.

## Practise
Use NeetCode Stack and Queue/Deque-related problems. Include parentheses validation, next-greater-element style problems, queue simulation and BFS applications.

## Check your understanding
- Why does a stack solve nested-bracket matching?
- When is a deque more useful than a queue?
- What invariant does a monotonic stack maintain?
- How can two stacks implement a queue, and what is the amortised cost?
